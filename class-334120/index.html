⚙️ Master the Mechanics

The rules are simple, but mastery requires deep thought:

  • Merge: When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value (e.g., 2+2=4).
  • Grid Limits: The game ends when the 4x4 grid is completely full and no more moves can be made.
  • Goal: Keep merging until you create a single tile with the number 2048.

Expert Strategies & Hacks for 2048

📐 The Corner Strategy

Always keep your highest value tile in one specific corner (e.g., bottom-right). This prevents smaller tiles from getting stuck behind your largest numbers.

🚫 Avoid One Direction

If your highest tile is in the bottom row, never press the 'Up' arrow unless absolutely necessary. Moving up can pull your highest tile out of its safe corner.

🧠 Think Ahead

Don't just swipe randomly. Treat it like chess—plan your moves two or three steps in advance to create a chain reaction of merges.
